Studio App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Studio App is a private tool used by Colours & Patterns Design Studio and its team members to manage projects, tasks, contacts, bills and approvals.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for access to your Google Drive. It uses this access only to create and read the studio's own folders ("Colours & Patterns" and "Colours & Patterns — Private"), to store project files such as photos, drawings and bills, and to sync the app's data between the studio's devices.

Where your data is stored

All app data is stored on your phone and in your own Google Drive. The app has no server. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Google user data

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features above, and its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your phone; files in your Drive stay under your control.
